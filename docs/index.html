How this dataset is built

Every record's title and abstract is classified by an ensemble of LLMs (GLM, Claude Sonnet, DeepSeek) against the health-financing-function taxonomy — Revenue raising, Pooling, Purchasing, Benefits decisions, Public financial management, plus recurrent and capital-investment financing — with a consensus step across models. Records that no model places in any bucket, or that stay Unclear after consensus, are screened out before full extraction.

Records that pass the bucket screen go through full field extraction: study design, type of analysis, data type and source, unit of observation, geography, outcome domain, and (for Revenue-raising studies) sub-questions on external/donor and non-governmental financing mechanisms. A DOI-recovery and MeSH/OpenAlex topic harvest runs on the extracted set.

Rules that keep the numbers honest

A study can carry more than one financing function or outcome domain and is counted once in every one it's tagged with — composition shares in the Explorer can add to more than 100%.

Multi-country studies count once per country in the country panels and the country profile; the "Studies in view" tile still counts each study once.

Extracted geography only, matched against a reused country reference table. A handful of real geographies named in the extraction — including Taiwan, Israel, Kazakhstan and Cuba — aren't in that reference table and are therefore visibly absent from country-level views (map, table, country profile) while still counted in every other chart.

"NA" classification values are dropped, not shown as a category — a record with no financing-function or outcome-domain consensus simply carries no tag on that dimension, rather than appearing as a fake "unknown" bucket. "Other" and "Unclear" are kept as real, visible categories.

Limitations

Classification is LLM-based with a cross-model consensus step, not independently human-verified. Geography is free text matched against a fixed country list, so genuine geographies outside that list are excluded from country-level figures (see above). DOI and MeSH/topic coverage are partial. Disease-burden and health-spending figures are country-level reference data, not something extracted from the studies — comparisons against them are ecological, not causal.
