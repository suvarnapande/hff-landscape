(Un)equal weight: Health financing research output compared to health expenditure

Measured against what health systems spend, the pattern reverses: Sub-Saharan Africa has the most health financing research per dollar (11.9 studies per US$1bn), 5× East Asia & Pacific (2.3). That reflects how little is spent there, not a large evidence base: in absolute terms North America is named by 17,233 studies and Sub-Saharan Africa by 4,193.
