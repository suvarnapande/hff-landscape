(Un)equal weight: Health financing research output compared to health expenditure

Measured against what health systems spend, the pattern reverses: Sub-Saharan Africa has the most health financing research per dollar (9.8 studies per US$1bn), 6× Latin America & Caribbean (1.6). That reflects how little is spent there, not a large evidence base: in absolute terms North America is named by 10,634 studies and Sub-Saharan Africa by 3,438.
