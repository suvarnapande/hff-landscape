(Un)equal weight: Which health financing functions research focuses on

Across all studies, benefits decisions is the financing function tagged most often. Country by country, benefits decisions leads in 49 of the 150 countries with a clear lead and revenue raising in 44. By region, benefits decisions leads in AFR, EUR, WPR; pooling leads in AMR, SEAR; revenue raising leads in EMR.
