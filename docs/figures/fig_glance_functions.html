Health financing research at a glance

In all, 44,122 studies (83%) named at least one country, giving 47,488 study-country pairs across 198 countries and territories. 29% of studies were about one country, the United States. Relative to population, research is densest in North America, Australia, New Zealand and parts of northern and western Europe, and thinnest in North Africa and the Middle East, parts of Central Africa and Central Asia.

Regions: WHO (AFR African, AMR Americas, SEAR South-East Asia, EUR European, EMR Eastern Mediterranean, WPR Western Pacific), as listed by the WHO Global Health Observatory; places that the WHO's country list does not assign to a region (Taiwan, Hong Kong, Kosovo) take the WHO region of their UN M49 sub-region. Financing functions: the HFF financing function groups (Other and Unclear left out); a study can carry more than one. Outcome: financial protection outcome domain. Map: studies per 10 million people (World Bank population), Robinson projection; grey = no study naming the country or no population data.
