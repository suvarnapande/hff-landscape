# HFF v2.5 data dictionary

Generated by `make_hff_codebook_v2_5.py` from `hff_analysis_dataset_v2_5.parquet` (70,230 rows x 123 columns; **53,414 included studies**, `analysis_include == TRUE`). Do not edit by hand: change the descriptions in the script and regenerate. The script fails if any column has no description.

**% present** = not null, not an empty string and not one of the literal missing sentinels `NA`, `Could not find`, `None`, `nan`; an array cell counts when non-empty. Two columns are given: among all rows (every record that entered the v2.2-v2.5 pool, plus the 51 v1 rows outside it) and among the included studies. Constant columns are flagged as carrying no information. Multi-valued fields are `; `-joined, except `financing_function_v52` / `financing_function_v52_pre_hta` (`;`, no space) and `geo_final` (comma-separated).

Things to know before using any column: v2.5 has the same rows as v2.2-v2.4 and 53,414 included studies: v2.4's 53,365 plus 49 records that describe themselves as an HTA, re-included as Benefits decisions (Decision 17; `hta_decision17`). `also_in_hee` marks the 1,874 included studies also included in the HEE arm: report the arms separately and never sum them. The bibliographic and topic columns cover every route (20,417 included October additions). The September-only columns still exist for v1 rows only (`origin == 'v1_include'`, 32,997 of the included, a cap of 61.8%). `function_label_source == 'second_opinion'` is a quality flag; `screening_generation` and `origin` are provenance only. See `../V2_5_DATASET_README.md`.

## Companion file: `hff_topic_coordinates_v2_5.csv`

One row per included study (53,414), joinable on `record_id`: 53,365 positioned by the fitted topic model and 49 (the Decision 17 HTA records) placed into the same map by nearest neighbours. Written by `make_hff_topic_coordinates_v2_5.py`; copied into the team export and the share folder. It holds the positions behind the topic maps drawn by `projects/hsf/analysis/R/fig-topicmap_v3.R` and `fig-topicmap-financing_v3.R` (`figures/fig_topicmap_v2_5.png`, `fig_topicmap_sizes_v2_5.png`, `fig_topicmap_financing_v2_5.png`). It supersedes `hff_topic_coordinates_v2_4.csv` (the 53,365 fitted studies only); the fitted positions are identical in both.

| column | description |
|---|---|
| `record_id` | EPPI-Reviewer record identifier, as in the analysis dataset. |
| `x`, `y` | 2-D UMAP position of the study's title + abstract embedding (all-MiniLM-L6-v2) from the v3 topic model; for placed studies, the median position of their 15 nearest fitted neighbours. **For drawing only: UMAP positions are not distances**, and neither axis has a meaning. |
| `topic_cluster_id` | HDBSCAN cluster, identical to `topic_cluster_id` in the dataset (checked); `-1` = unclustered noise (13,612 studies, 25.5%; 16 of the 49 placed). |
| `topic_cluster_label` | c-TF-IDF label; `Unclustered (noise)` for `-1`. |
| `topic_placement` | `fitted` (53,365) or `nearest-neighbour, k=15` (49); identical to the dataset column. |

**Provenance and caveats.** The model (`projects/hsf/analysis/R/05-topic-model_v3.py`) was **fitted on the 53,782 studies included in v2.3**, before the v2.4 DOI de-duplication, and was not refitted. The 53,365 fitted v2.5 studies keep the position and cluster they were fitted with. The 49 studies re-included by Decision 17 were not in the fit and were **placed** (L. Sempe, 2026-10-14): embedded with the same model, matched to their 15 nearest fitted v2.4 studies by cosine similarity, positioned at the neighbours' median coordinates and given the neighbours' most common cluster (ties: the nearer neighbour). No fitted point or cluster moves. Cluster sizes from the fit (`topic_cluster_n`, `topic_labels_v3.csv`) count the 53,782: **recount them on the 53,414** for any v2.5 figure or table (they differ for 43 of the 56 clusters; on the 53,414 the clusters hold 198 to 6,299 studies).
